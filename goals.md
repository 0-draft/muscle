# Goals

The weekly review checks progress against this file.

## Process goals

- Log every training session.
- Weigh in at least 6 days a week.
- Measure the waist every 4 weeks.

## Outcome goals (12 weeks from 2026-09-23)

- Set after the first 2 weeks of logging, once there's a baseline. Example: bench press e1RM +5 kg, body weight +0.25% per week.
