## Bottom line

At 70kg, aim for **112–154g** a day (1.6–2.2 g/kg). Split it across 4 or more meals of about 28–39g each. In a cut, go toward the top of the range or higher.

## Daily total

> [!A] Lean mass gains from resistance training plateau at about **1.6 g/kg/day** of protein. To cover individual variation, **2.2 g/kg** is a reasonable upper bound.

- In a meta-regression of 49 RCTs (n=1,863), the plateau was at 1.62 g/kg, with a 95% confidence interval of 1.03–2.20 g/kg (Morton 2018)
- A meta-analysis of 74 RCTs also recommends 1.6 g/kg or more for people under 65 (Nunes 2022)
- The ISSN position stand recommends 1.4–2.0 g/kg, rising to **2.3–3.1 g/kg** for trained people in a cut (Jäger 2017)

The exact plateau is still unclear. Treat 1.6 as the floor and 2.2 as the default when unsure.

## Dose per meal and number of meals

> [!C] About **0.4 g/kg (28g)** per meal, spread over **4 or more meals a day**, leaves little on the table.

The "you can only absorb 30g at a time" claim has been refuted. In a study where subjects ate 100g after training, the synthesis response was larger and lasted longer (12+ hours) than with 25g, with no upper limit in sight (Trommelen 2023).

> [!C] A large single dose is not wasted. Whether that leads to different long-term hypertrophy is still unknown.

Trommelen 2023 measured the acute response to one meal, in young men with 12 per group. It did not measure differences in muscle mass over months. So splitting or bunching your protein matters less than the daily total.

## Protein before bed

> [!C] Having **30–40g** (casein or similar) before bed may add some hypertrophy. But the effect may just come from higher total daily intake.

- In a 12-week RCT, the group that took 27.5g of protein before bed had larger gains in quadriceps cross-sectional area (+8.4 vs +4.8 cm², Snijders 2015)
- But the control group got a zero-calorie placebo, so the result may only reflect a difference in total intake
- A systematic review (Reis 2021) also supports an effect in young men

In practice, treat it as a convenient way to hit your daily total.
